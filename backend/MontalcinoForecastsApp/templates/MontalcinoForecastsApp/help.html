Welcome to Montalcino Forecasts App

Montalcino Forecasts App is a website for forecasting the quality of Brunello di Montalcino vintages based on weather data collected in the Montalcino area.

The database contains daily data on temperature, rainfall, humidity, pressure, wind, solar radiation and dew point. To generate forecasts you need to define a model (in this case linear regression), the climatic indices it will use to compute the quality, and the date range used to train the model.

The site is divided into two sections, described below.

Simplified app

The simplified version of the app automatically determines the best climatic indices to use for the forecasts. You need to choose the period of data to use for training (the longest one is selected by default) and pick one of the proposed options.

With the chosen model you can then forecast the quality of a vintage.

Full app

The full version lets you manually select any combination of climatic indices as regressors for the linear regression and choose the date range used for training.

A summary screen is then shown with the coefficient of determination (R2), a chart comparing the vintages used for training with the values predicted for those years, and, for each regressor, its coefficient and p-value.

After training you can use the model to forecast the quality of any vintage.

Climatic indices

To train the model you need to select one or more climatic indices to use for the prediction. They give a concise indication of the weather conditions that occurred during the year. Many of them are self-explanatory (e.g. "Average pressure between April and October"), while for the others we give a short definition here:

  • GST (Growing Season Temperature): is the average temperature of the vine's growing season, i.e. April-October.
  • Winkler Index: considering the daily average temperatures between April and October above 10°C, the Winkler index is the sum of the differences between these temperatures and 10°C. The formula used is
    $$WI = \sum_{1 April}^{31 October} max((T_{avg} - 10\text{°}C), 0)$$
  • Huglin Index: is the product of a coefficient K, which depends on latitude and equals 1.03 for Montalcino, and the sum, from April to September, of the mean of the daily average temperature and the daily maximum temperature minus 10°C. The formula used is
    $$HI = K \cdot \sum_{1 April}^{30 September} (\frac{T_{avg} + T_{max}}{2} - 10\text{°}C)$$
