GST (Growing Season Temperature)
The average temperature of the vine's growing season, i.e. April-October.
More details
Winkler Index
Considering the daily average temperatures between April and October above 10°C, the Winkler index is the sum of the differences between these temperatures and 10°C.
More details
Huglin Index
The product of a coefficient K, which depends on latitude and equals 1.03 for Montalcino, and the sum, from April to September, of the mean of the daily average temperature and the daily maximum temperature minus 10°C.
